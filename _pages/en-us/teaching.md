### Teaching Philosophy

My teaching aims to create a stimulating, interactive, and inclusive learning environment, where every student feels valued and able to contribute. I draw on varied methods — quizzes, group discussions, case studies, simulations, collaborative projects — that foster active engagement and critical thinking, grounded in problem-based and experiential learning to prepare students for real-world academic and professional challenges.

### Mentoring Philosophy

My approach to mentoring rests on a simple principle: creating an environment where everyone feels heard, included, and supported. I focus on understanding what each student brings, tailoring projects to their interests, and helping them build a researcher identity — giving them the tools they need while fostering independence. This relationship is sustained through regular check-ins, where listening and mutual adjustment matter as much as the results themselves.
